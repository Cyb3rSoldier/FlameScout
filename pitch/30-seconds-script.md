# FlameScout — "30 Seconds of Glory" Script

**Use for:** the 30-second global-judging video — the demo NASA judges see on the project page.
**Word budget:** ~68 spoken words @ crisp delivery — nothing extra earns its place.
**Hard rules (NASA's):** 30 s maximum · **English subtitles required** · host externally (YouTube) and make the link public — no logins · **you cannot upload it to the NASA site itself**. Target under **29.5 s** in the edit.

---

## 0:00–0:05 · HOOK — Team + one line (~11 words)

> [Fast, confident, straight to camera]
>
> We're **[SLOT: team name]**, from **[SLOT: city]** — and this flame is in freefall.

**[ON SCREEN: FreefallFlame morph animation; team logo lower-third]**

---

## 0:05–0:12 · PROBLEM — One concrete fact, no essay (~15 words)

> The challenge you answered:
>
> In microgravity, flames stop rising — and where crews will live, published fire data is **empty**.

**[ON SCREEN: Coverage Map heatmap, one-second zoom on the empty Moon corner — the same image judges saw in the 240s video, now a single flash]**

---

## 0:12–0:22 · THE THING WORKING — Show the build (~23 words)

> [SCREEN RECORDING — this is the half judges remember. No narration slippage: the words land as the actions land.]
>
> **FlameScout, live.** It ranks NASA PSI experiments for a lunar habitat, predicts from **280 real FLEX tests** — or says **"not enough data."**

**[ON SCREEN: screen recording — Mission Fit ranking scroll (~3 s) → Predict result row (~3 s) → "Not enough data" badge (~3 s); dataset caption held ≥2 s: `NASA PSI · FLEX Table IX · NASA/TP-2015-216046`]**

⚠ **Dataset name must be visible on screen while it runs** — put it in the caption bar, not just the voiceover.

---

## 0:22–0:30 · IMPACT — Who it helps, end on the name (~16 words)

> Safer Artemis-era habitats — and better fire safety here on Earth — one honest answer at a time. **This. Is. FlameScout.**

**[ON SCREEN: ISS → Moon → Earth graphic sweep → logo end-card with project name, ≥3 s frozen so judges at second 30 still see it]**

---

## Timing & delivery check

| Beat | Target | Words | Cut if hot |
|---|---|---|---|
| Hook | 5 s | ~11 | Keep — it is the team rule |
| Problem | 7 s | ~15 | Keep the word "empty" — cut everything else |
| Build | 10 s | ~23 | Drop "live" or the lunar-habitat phrase, keep the FLEX number |
| Impact | 8 s | ~16 | Never cut the final three words |
| **Total** | **~29.5 s** | **~65** | |

- One voiceover throughout — **no music competing with speech** (subtitles carry it anyway)
- Reserve the last 0.5 s as pure logo frame — judges are "not required to watch past second 30", so the final image must be the name
- Subtitles burned in, English, synced to the four beats
- Same voice, same team faces, same hook line as the 240 s video — the two videos should feel like one campaign
